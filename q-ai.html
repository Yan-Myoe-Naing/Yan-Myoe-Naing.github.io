Q AI

A checking layer for answers from an AI assistant.

A confident answer can still quote an old procedure or miss a required check. Our prototype gives staff a clear verdict, shows the evidence, and brings a person in when the answer cannot be cleared.

Trusted AI Agents Team project Working prototype

01 / The problem

The answer sounded right.

In the demo, a simulated bank assistant gives an overseas transfer limit from an outdated procedure. It also leaves out a scam-risk check for a first-time payee. Both errors matter before an employee acts on the answer.

02 / The approach

Check before and after the answer.

  1. Clarify the question. Q AI asks for missing details and stops unsafe requests before the assistant responds.
  2. Check the answer. Rules and AI checks compare claims, citations and question coverage with current synthetic procedures. Higher-risk answers get a second opinion.
  3. Make the decision visible. Staff see Verified, Unconfirmed or Conflicts with policy, along with reasons. Unresolved answers go to a human reviewer.

03 / My contribution

My contributions

I represented Singapore Polytechnic at OCBC Ignite as part of Team Quadrant. I proposed checking prompts before the assistant responds, giving each answer a verdict, and retraining the model.

I redesigned the interface around the verdict and its evidence, built contextual follow-up handling, and worked on the second-opinion checks for higher-risk answers.
