Catch & Go

Where should a location-based game run its first pilot?

Our team analysed a sample of 200 Singapore residents for a fictional game launch. We compared MRT areas on gaming intensity, income readiness, gaming motivation, transit accessibility, and age fit.

My contribution

From player motivations to a pilot recommendation

I built the Power BI visuals and analysis for Rising Insight 3, which ranked MRT areas by frequent gamers who played for well-being-related reasons. I also built the introduction, setting, hook, final synthesis, solution and next steps, and references pages. My teammates led Rising Insights 1, 2, 4, and 5.

Findings

Start in Hougang

Hougang, Choa Chu Kang, and Sengkang tied for first on the motivation measure, with three matching residents each. Across all five criteria, our team recommended Hougang for the initial pilot and Yishun for a possible expansion. We proposed tracking encounters, repeat visits, battle activity, and player feedback before expanding.

The findings describe the survey sample of 200 residents.
