Where should a location-based game run its first pilot?

Our team analysed a sample of 200 Singapore residents for a fictional game launch. We compared MRT areas on gaming intensity, income readiness, gaming motivation, transit accessibility, and age fit.

Rising Insight 3

Where do motivated players already live?

Of the 62 residents who played at least seven hours a week, 30 named a well-being-related reason for gaming (48%). Hougang was one of three MRT areas tied for the highest count on this measure.

Methods & tools

How I narrowed the sample

  1. Filter the audience in Power BI. I used the survey’s seven-or-more-hours gaming bands and selected three motivations: relieving stress, immersing in another world, and feeling accomplished.
  2. Measure and compare. I used a DAX measure to show how many frequent gamers met the motivation criteria as a percentage. I grouped matching residents by nearest MRT and built a column chart to compare the counts.
  3. Check the geographic spread. I used Azure Maps in Power BI to check whether those MRT areas formed a cluster. The leading areas were spread across western, north-eastern, and eastern Singapore.

I built Rising Insight 3 and the report’s introduction, setting, hook, synthesis, solution and next steps, and references. My teammates led Rising Insights 1, 2, 4, and 5.

Findings

Start in Hougang

The tie made motivation useful for shortlisting. I brought that result together with the team’s findings on gaming intensity, income, transit access, and age. Hougang performed consistently across the criteria, so we recommended starting around its MRT-linked public spaces.

We proposed tracking encounters, repeat visits, battle activity, and player feedback before deciding whether to expand to Yishun.
